ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Building the Future with Technology

The Department of Computer Applications is dedicated to preparing students for success in the fast-changing world of software and technology.

The department focuses on theoretical foundations and practical skills in programming, database management, software development and system design.

Students are also exposed to modern technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Mobile App Development, Cybersecurity and Cloud Computing.

Learning is supported through projects, coding laboratories, internships, workshops and industry exposure.

Our Vision

To emerge as a renowned center in the field of computer applications by providing quality education and research and producing globally competent professionals with ethical values.

Our Mission

  • Provide high-quality education with a modern curriculum.
  • Promote innovation and research.
  • Develop ethical and socially responsible professionals.
  • Build industry-academia relationships.
